About

Hi! I'm Rupak Tiwari, an AI Engineer and Researcher based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I build LLM, RAG and machine learning systems, and my research focuses on low-resource speech recognition and machine translation for languages of Nepal, including Nepali, Tamang and Nepal Bhasha (Newari).

I have worked as a Junior Research Assistant at the Information and Language Processing Research Lab (ILPRL), Kathmandu University, where I work on a Tamang–Nepali–English tri-lingual machine translation system funded by Google Academic Research. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Kathmandu University.

Interests: Speech Recognition · Machine Translation · LLMs & RAG · Low-resource NLP · ML Systems
